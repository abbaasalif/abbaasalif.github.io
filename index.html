Selected work

Industry work

When the assistant understands but doesn’t help

I work on two parts of a conversation: recognizing what someone wants and checking whether the response helped them do it. I built evaluation tools that let reviewers distinguish a misunderstood request from an incomplete answer. That distinction points to two different fixes: change how the request is interpreted, or change what happens next.

Video understanding

REVEAL: Did the model watch the video?

What happens when a familiar action plays backward? In REVEAL, my co-authors and I tested whether video-language models followed the action on screen or described what they expected to happen. Some models described the forward action even when the clip was reversed.

Published scope: Five controlled tests across 12 video-language models, covering failures such as weak cross-frame integration and reliance on language cues.

Read the REVEAL paper

Computer vision

Revelio: Sending data from a screen to a phone

My co-authors and I built a way to embed information in video without adding a visible code to the picture. A standard smartphone camera captures the screen, and a neural decoder recovers the message even when the screen and camera are not synchronized.

Published result: Zero decoding errors across four videos at distances up to 2 m in direct-view tests.

Read the Revelio paper
